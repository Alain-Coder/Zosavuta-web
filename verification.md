## Organizer Verification / KYC

Before an Organizer or Attendee_Organizer in the organizer page can publish an event,
sell tickets, generate physical tickets, or receive seller proceeds,
the account must complete the platform's organizer verification process. In the verification process they should either choose between unregistered or registered business/organization. If unregistered show and collect the individual Organizer verfication fields and hide the registered business/organization fields. If registered business/organization show and collect the registered business/organization fields and hide the individual Organizer verfication fields.

### Individual Organizer

Collect:

- Full legal name
- Date of birth
- National ID or Passport  upload (image or pdf)  (Notice for it to be clear on the edges and all corners are visible)
- Phone number
- postal address
- Bank/mobile-money payout details

### Verification Status

Organizer verification should use statuses:

- NOT_STARTED
- SUBMITTED
- UNDER_REVIEW
- APPROVED
- REJECTED
- SUSPENDED

An Organizer must not be allowed to publish events, sell tickets,
generate physical selling-point tickets, or receive seller payouts
until their verification status is APPROVED.

Administrators/accountants with the appropriate permissions should
be able to review submitted KYC information, approve/reject it,
request additional information, and view the verification history.

All verification decisions must be audit logged.
